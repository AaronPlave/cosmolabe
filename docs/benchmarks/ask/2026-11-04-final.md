## How it is judged

There are 22 user-level tasks across six catalogs: Voyagers, Cassini SOI, Psyche, Ingenuity/Jezero, Earth–Moon and MoonFall/Shackleton. Each runs in a fresh scene and a fresh conversation. The tasks include:

- **Literal requests:** the issue's own wording.
- **Paraphrases:** "Voyager 2's closest pass by Neptune", "when did Cassini get nearest to Titan".
- **Search criteria:** a 10,000 km search threshold, and "all flybys" over the whole coverage.
- **Refusals:** there is no flyby to show (Voyager 1/Neptune), or the body is not in the scene (Jupiter in the Psyche scene).
- **Other operations:** terrain styling, a pin then a saved point, a measurement, a bookmark, an instrument feed, a display layer, an activity seek, a synthetic landing site.
- **Multi-turn:** the five-turn close-up, and a selection replaced while the pause is kept.
- **Approval declined:** the harness declines the approval prompt through the panel's own approval path.
- **Read-only:** an inventory that must change nothing.
- **Ambiguity:** an ambiguous "that viewpoint".

**State** is judged by typed predicates evaluated over reads the harness makes itself, and camera composition by the harness's own projection of the render camera. Closest approaches are compared with independent range sampling, with every coarse minimum refined. "All" is compared with an independent count of minima over the pair's whole kernel coverage.

**Answers** are judged by the harness's own checks:
- stated distances against truth or validated published numbers;
- stated dates against truth;
- flyby claims about the subject;
- the count of minima;
- refusals, the declined-deletion statement, the synthetic-site disclosure, and asking when the request is ambiguous.

**False success** means the state failed while the answer did not say so, or the answer stated a fact the truth contradicts.

**Changes to the judge after seeing results.** These were made after reading the runs, on the evidence of the answers. They are listed so the strict numbers stay visible: before them, Sonnet scored 43/46 and Haiku 108/115.
- A quoted catalog name ("V2 Neptune (1989-08-25)") is not a date or flyby claim.
- A question ("Do you want me to show that encounter?") is not a claim.
- A true statement about Voyager 2's flyby, within two sentences of naming Voyager 2, is not a claim about Voyager 1.
- "Nothing was removed" counts as saying the deletion did not happen.

## What changed to get here

The design changes in this pass are in [agent-tools.md](../../agent-tools.md#operation-contracts):
- **Operation contracts:** every operation declares what it changes and how its result is observed. They replace the name lists, the readback list and the four goal kinds.
- **Typed goals:** goals are typed predicates the loop holds and checks.
- **Answers after the check:** an answer is shown only after that check, and must agree with the published facts.
- **Flyby classification:** a closest approach counts as a flyby only inside the sphere of influence of whichever participant has one.

Problems the pilots found, all fixed before the final runs:

| Found | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Haiku sent numbers as text, arrays as JSON text, optional fields as `null`, and a JSON parameter as the object itself | `normalizeInput` undoes exactly those before strict validation |
| A tool call cut off at the output limit ended the request | One shorter retry; nothing in the cut-off call ran |
| Observer and target swapped, so no encounter criterion was found | Classify by whichever participant has a sphere of influence |
| A second search made the first look undone | `eventRun` reads the query's own results |
| Goals stated over reads that do not exist | Refused at `checkGoal` |
| Cassini–Titan coverage is two windows, and a search must fit one | The guide says to search each window and compare |
| Pins on the reference ellipsoid instead of the ground | The ground (`rendered`) is listed first and described as the default |
| A coarse search step missed some of Psyche–Earth's minima | The guide says to keep the kind's default step |

## Offline gate (PR CI)

- **`operation-contracts.test.ts`:**
  - every operation has a contract;
  - every write classifies what it changes;
  - a host that reports success without changing state is never `completed`;
  - the unobservable writes are a pinned list.
- **`ask-engine.test.ts`:**
  - answers are held until checked;
  - flyby and fact contradictions are corrected, or replaced by the host's account;
  - a stated encounter stops a wrong presentation before it runs;
  - camera and clock takeover, and scene reload;
  - a newly registered operation is offered, guided and verified, then removed;
  - a withheld built-in loses its tool and guidance.
- **Other suites:**
  - `outcome.test.ts`: predicate checks against false positives.
  - `ask-reliability.test.ts`: the five-turn conversation.
  - `encounter.test.ts`: flyby classification.
  - `reliability.test.mjs`: the harness's own judging.
