# Ask benchmark, 2026-11-01

Keep fixed tools as the default. Haiku was materially cheaper in this exploratory
sample, but neither model/mode had complete factual acceptance. This does not
establish general equivalence or justify changing production model routing.

[Reproduction and scoring](README.md) and [compact aggregate/pose fixture](2026-11-01-summary.json).

## Quality before cost

The quality denominator is **twelve tasks, 48 cells**: eleven tasks from the
original matrix plus the corrected missing-viewpoint case. Each used a fresh
scene/conversation. “Show the next flight” is excluded: the frozen current clock
is a plausible implicit anchor, so its unchanged-state rubric was invalid. The
harness retains it as a diagnostic, automatically excluded from quality and
comparable-suite usage totals. Supplementary repeats and invalid airfield probes
are also excluded.

| Model | Tools | Scene checks | Factual pass / partial / fail | Both |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Sonnet 5.5 | Fixed | 12/12 | 10 / 1 / 1 | 10/12 |
| Sonnet 5.5 | Discovery | 12/12 | 11 / 1 / 0 | 11/12 |
| Haiku 5.5 | Fixed | 12/12 | 11 / 1 / 0 | 11/12 |
| Haiku 5.5 | Discovery | 12/12 | 8 / 3 / 1 | 8/12 |

“Both” requires independent scene checks and a factual pass. Correct capability
refusal and forced cancellation are accepted under their own rubrics, without
claiming the requested mutation completed. Factual reviews were by one Codex
agent, neither blinded nor multiple-human ratings.

All four modes reached Flight 12 at its paused estimated start; independent
render-camera projection/size checks confirmed framing. All four mixed-workflow
answers omitted full imagery attribution and degraded-tile caveats. Dedicated
Mars-inventory answers improved: both Sonnet modes and fixed Haiku reported all
attributions and degraded/missing tile counts; discovery Haiku omitted the latter.

All modes selected the exact synthetic MoonFall PSR waypoint and identified demo
provenance. Sonnet fixed changed longitude -2° to “2°E”. Haiku discovery inferred
large distance from a longitude difference near the pole. The two catalog anchors
(-2°, -89.71°) and (129.78°, -89.67°) are about 17.17 km apart on the catalog's
1737.4 km sphere in MOON_ME_DE421; this is not terrain or crater-boundary distance.

The historical Lunar Orbit and mixed-workflow snapshots now pass a full
position/target/up comparison against the catalog viewpoint (1 km position/target,
0.001 unit-vector tolerance). All four landing snapshots also pass a catalog-anchor
target check within 0.5 km and eye distance of 0.1–5 km. The anchor was independently
resolved in the loaded viewer at the recorded epoch, transformed through the Moon's
body pose; the tolerance allows asynchronous terrain refinement. The compact fixture
retains one identical pose per task and the four-sample counts. The new live probe
was checked before/after navigation: the initial view failed, the destination passed.
Selection alone and radius alone cannot pass these new checks.

All four lunar-provenance answers preserved unknown coverage/withheld metadata;
all refused unavailable persistent measurement without scene changes. All four
cancel tests stopped at the next HTTP boundary after a write, retaining applied
changes without later mutations. This covers that boundary, not every in-flight
race. The corrected “Show that viewpoint” case passed all four modes: each asked
which real viewpoint was intended without changing scene state.

## Efficiency and evidence limits

Fixed tools started at 13,368 schema characters versus discovery's 2,500, but
discovery used more total work. In the original twelve-task matrix **including
the excluded next-flight diagnostic**, Sonnet discovery described 44 capability IDs
with no within-task repeats; Haiku described 43 with one repeat. Scene reads were
21/21 for Sonnet fixed/discovery and 22/24 for Haiku. These diagnostic counts are
not the twelve-task quality denominator above.

The 6,000-character tool serialization budget omitted an initial Ingenuity scene
(~6,200 compact characters before receipt overhead). Underlying tool success and
retained pre-serialization observations do not prove model receipt. The historical
matrix lacks per-call serialized-result evidence: its scene checks and manual
answer reviews do **not** establish successful model-visible inventory delivery.
The updated harness checks actual serialized results keyed by call ID; missing,
partial and `result-too-large` payloads cannot pass `inventoryRead`.

Four instrumented Flight 12 repeats confirmed independent reads already group in
model responses, though execution remains sequential. Requests were 5/9 for
Sonnet fixed/discovery and 6/13 for Haiku; oversized results were 1/1 and 1/2.
Haiku discovery repeated an unchanged oversized scene read before mutation.
Future scene compaction should preserve core state while targeted dataset reads
retain attribution, unknown coverage, degraded counts and partial receipts.
Compaction, runtime concurrency and #297 feature work remain separate.

The original matrix used `bbc5cb3b`, instrumented repeats `69280f25`, and corrected
viewpoint runs `ff4517a9`. Full one-off reports, answers, observations and attached
review digests are deliberately omitted from this PR after summarization; there
is no claimed external archive or independently auditable raw-answer review bundle.
The small fixture retains aggregates and representative camera evidence only.
New runs write reports outside the repository; keep exact reports and matching
review digests together in an intentionally maintained external archive if needed.
A next decision should use a small repeated high-value Haiku fixed/Sonnet fixed
comparison, without committing another full run dump.
